You don’t have to say it! Morse says it in code.

What Morse Does

Type any short phrase (up to 40 characters) or pick a preset. Morse flashes it with his eyes — in the shape and color you choose. Leave the page open on your desk or phone and he’ll keep transmitting while you’re away.

Sample Uses

Status & Boundaries

On Break · Back Soon · Go Away · Do Not Disturb · In a Meeting · Lunch

Playful / Sass

Fuck Off · Not Now · I’m Not Cheap · Blame Morse · Silent Sass · Ask Again Later

Seasonal & Social

Merry Christmas · Happy Birthday · Welcome · Congrats · Good Luck

Exaggerated Distress

SOS (no, seriously, HELP) · Send Coffee · Save Me · Emergency: Boredom

How the eyes work

Choose from six eye shapes and any color. Optional letter display shows the current character to the side while Morse flashes. Mouth style can be set to smile, neutral, frown, sneer, or “oh.”

Away-from-desk mode

Leave Morse running full-screen or as a persistent tab. He loops your chosen phrase so the message stays visible even when you’ve stepped away.

Add to Home Screen

On your phone, open this page in the browser and choose “Add to Home Screen.” You’ll get an icon that launches Morse instantly — no app store, no download.

Morse is free and runs entirely in your browser.
